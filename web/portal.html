What we store

          

            
Metadata onlyYour e-mail, and how you sign in: Google, or a password kept only as a hash. For each key: its name, prefix, environment, expiry and a SHA-256 hash. For each decision your middleware reports: a hash of the session id, resource name, decision, actor, connection state, detected tool names and reason codes. Nothing else: no payloads, no identities, no IPs.
